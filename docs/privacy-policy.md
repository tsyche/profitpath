# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 2026

## Overview

ProfitPath is a client-side application that runs entirely on your device. Your business data — scenarios, prices, costs, calculations — is never collected, transmitted, or stored on any server. The only thing that leaves your device is an anonymous count of page loads, described below.

## Your Business Data

ProfitPath does **not**:
- Collect personal information
- Send your scenarios, prices, costs, or calculations to any server
- Store your scenarios anywhere but your own device
- Display advertisements

All your business data remains on your device, in your browser's local storage, unless you explicitly export or share it yourself.

## Site Usage Counting

To gauge whether the app is actually being used — and roughly how much — ProfitPath includes [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/), a privacy-focused, no-cookie visit counter, on both the web app and the Android app (APK). Each page load reports:

- The page path (tagged `/web` or `/apk` so the two can be told apart)
- Referrer, browser/OS, and screen size
- Country, derived transiently from IP address — the IP itself is not stored

This is a **count of visits, not a record of usage** — it does not know what you did in the app, what values you entered, or which features you used. It:

- Uses no cookies and does no fingerprinting
- Automatically honors your browser's Do Not Track setting
- Is skipped entirely on local development servers

The counter data is visible only to the app's developer, purely to answer "is anyone using this, and how much." It is unrelated to the separate in-app feature-usage analytics described below.

## In-App Feature Analytics (opt-in)

ProfitPath includes a separate, **opt-in, local-only** analytics feature (toggle in Settings) for the developer's own product-development insight. When enabled, it records which features you use — never your business data — and stores that log in your browser's local storage. It is **never transmitted anywhere**; it exists purely so you (or the developer, if you choose to export and share it) can see feature usage. It's off by default.

## Data Storage

- Scenarios are saved to your device's local storage only
- No cloud backup or synchronization
- You retain full control of your data at all times

## Third-Party Services

The only third-party service ProfitPath integrates with is GoatCounter, for the anonymous visit counting described above. ProfitPath does not use Google Analytics, advertising trackers, or any service that profiles individual users.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. Changes will be reflected here with an updated "Last Updated" date.
