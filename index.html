Capacity resources (optional)

Staff hours are one pool. Add a booth, room, or bay with its own weekly hours, then tag the offerings that use it. A tagged offering uses both staff time and the resource; Outputs shows whichever fills first as the bottleneck. Tick Staff-free for a self-serve resource: tagged offerings then draw no staff hours.

Ranges & confidence (optional)
Guessing fill or client counts? Enter a low and high; Outputs shows the min and max across all lows together and all highs together. Leave blank for no range. Tag how much you trust each offering's numbers and jot a source or caveat.

Group runs (optional)
A run is one cohort going through its Sessions / yr. Enter what one run costs you regardless of how full it is (room hire, hired instructor, consumables); Outputs charges it once per run. Break-even shows how many seats a run needs to cover that cost.
